Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains what All Women Painting does with information when you visit this website or contact us. The short version: we keep it simple, we only use what you send us to answer you and do the job, and we do not sell your information.

What this website collects

This website does not have accounts, does not use advertising or analytics trackers, and does not set cookies. The estimate form on the Contact page does not send anything to us. It builds a text message on your own device, and nothing is sent until you choose to text or call us yourself.

Information you give us

When you call or text us, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us, such as your name, address, the work you want done, and any photos you send. We use this to reply to you, schedule a visit, give you an estimate, and carry out the job. If you become a customer, we keep records of the job (such as the estimate, schedule, and invoice) for as long as we need them for our business, taxes, and warranty questions.

Photos you send of your home are used only to prepare your estimate and to plan the work. We do not post them on this website or social media without asking you first.

Third-party services this site uses

  • Google Fonts. The fonts on this site load from Google. When the page loads, Google receives your IP address and browser details, as it does for any site that uses its fonts. See Google’s privacy policy for how it handles that.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare). The animated 3D logo uses a code library delivered by cdnjs, which receives similar standard connection details.
  • Hosting. Our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and pages requested, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Your phone carrier. Calls and texts go through your carrier, and standard message and data rates may apply.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. We may share it with people who help us do your job (for example, a subcontractor working on your project), with service providers we use to run the business, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we need to keep it for tax or legal reasons. If you get texts from us and want them to stop, reply STOP or just tell us. Call us at 215-868-4152 for any of this.

Children

This website is for adults hiring painting services. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we add things like online chat, analytics, or forms that send information to us, we will update this page first. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 26, 2026

All Women Painting wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, voice control, or screen magnification. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we have done

  • The whole site works with a keyboard alone, including the photo viewer, and keyboard focus is always visible.
  • A “Skip to main content” link is the first thing a keyboard or screen reader user reaches.
  • Pages use headings, landmarks, and labeled form fields so screen readers can navigate them, and the page title changes when you move between sections.
  • Every photo has a text description. The decorative 3D logo is described once and its motion can be paused with the “Pause motion” button. It is also off automatically if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Text and buttons have strong color contrast in both light and dark modes, and the site follows your device’s light or dark setting.
  • The layout adapts to small screens and large text sizes, and works when you zoom in.
  • You can always reach us by phone or text instead of using the website.

Known limitations

Some of our job photos are small and low resolution, and the before and after photos do not include a full written description beyond their captions. We are working to improve these. We test with automated tools and by keyboard, and we welcome feedback if you find something that does not work for you.

Need help or an alternative?

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or want the information in another way, call or text us at 215-868-4152 and we will help right away. Tell us what page or feature gave you trouble and we will work to fix it.
